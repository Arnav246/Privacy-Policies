Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 25, 2026

Neon Drift ("the Application") is developed by Arnav Labs. This Application is a family-friendly arcade game designed for users of all ages.

1. Information Collection and Use

Neon Drift is built with a "Privacy by Design" approach. We do not collect, store, or share any personal information.

Local Storage: The Application saves your High Scores, Game Settings, and Tutorial progress locally on your device. This data is used solely for the functionality of the game and never leaves your device.

2. Third-Party Services and Advertising

To support the development of this free application, we use Google AdMob to display advertisements.

Families Policy Compliance: Because Neon Drift is rated for ages 3 and up, we have configured the AdMob SDK to treat all ad requests as Child-Directed. This means:

• Interest-based advertising is disabled.

• The use of personal data for ad targeting is restricted.

• All ad content is filtered to a "G" (General) rating suitable for all audiences.

3. Advertising Identifiers

The Application utilizes the Google Advertising ID solely for the purpose of ad delivery and rewarding user actions (such as the "Continue" feature). This identifier is handled by the Google Mobile Ads SDK in compliance with COPPA and Google Play's Families Policy.

4. Permissions

The Application requires minimal permissions:

• Internet Access: Necessary to load advertisements and verify rewarded ad completion.

• AD_ID Permission: Required for the AdMob SDK to function on Android 13+.

• Wake Lock: Used to keep the screen active during gameplay.

5. Children's Privacy

Neon Drift is fully compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We do not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13.

Our advertising partners are restricted from collecting personal information for behavioral targeting within this Application.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Since we do not collect user contact info, we encourage you to review this page periodically for any changes.
