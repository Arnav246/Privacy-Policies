Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 26, 2026

Neon Drift ("the Application") is developed by Arnav Labs. This Application is an arcade game designed for users aged 13 and above.

1. Information Collection and Use

Neon Drift is built with a "Privacy by Design" approach. We do not collect, store, or share any personal information.

Local Storage: The Application saves your High Scores, Game Settings, and Tutorial progress locally on your device. This data is used solely for the functionality of the game and never leaves your device.

2. Third-Party Services and Advertising

To support the development of this free application, we use Google AdMob to display advertisements.

Advertising Content: Advertisements are provided through Google AdMob. While we do not control the individual ads shown, we have configured ad settings to display age-appropriate advertising for users aged 13 and above. Ad content is subject to Google's advertising policies and may vary depending on availability and region.

3. Advertising Identifiers

The Application utilizes the Google Advertising ID solely for the purpose of ad delivery and rewarding user actions (such as the "Continue" feature). This identifier is handled by the Google Mobile Ads SDK in accordance with applicable Google Play policies.

4. Permissions

The Application requires minimal permissions:

• Internet Access: Necessary to load advertisements and verify rewarded ad completion.

• AD_ID Permission: Required for the AdMob SDK to function on Android 13+.

• Wake Lock: Used to keep the screen active during gameplay.

5. User Privacy

Neon Drift does not knowingly collect, store, or share personally identifiable information from its users. Any advertising-related data processing is performed by Google AdMob in accordance with its own privacy practices and applicable laws.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Since we do not collect user contact info, we encourage you to review this page periodically for any changes.
