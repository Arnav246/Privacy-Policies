Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28th May, 2026

Neon Drift ("the Application") is developed as a commercial-grade application but is provided at no cost.

This Application is designed to be played completely offline and does not require a network connection for core gameplay.

1. Information Collection and Use

Neon Drift does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information, location data, or usage analytics.

Local Storage: The Application saves your High Scores and Game Settings locally on your device using browser localStorage via WebView.

This data remains on your device and is never uploaded to external servers.

2. Third-Party Services

The Application does not utilize any third-party SDKs, tracking tools, or advertising networks.

There are no advertisements displayed within the app.

3. Permissions

The Application requires minimal permissions to function.

Internet (Optional): While the game is offline, standard WebView permissions may exist to handle external links such as developer support links, but no user data is shared through these.

4. Children's Privacy

Since the Application does not collect any personal data, it is inherently compliant with privacy regulations such as COPPA and GDPR.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time.

Since we do not collect user information, we cannot notify you directly; please review this page periodically for any changes.
